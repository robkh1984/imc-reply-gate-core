A cell that recommends nothing (None, No change) passes. A row whose Recommendation opens DECISION: is SUPPORTED only if it says what is unknown and why it cannot be measured, and no evidence.log line already measures it. Open the RECOMMENDATION-CHECK line with SUPPORTED only if every cell passes; otherwise open it with NOT SUPPORTED and name each failing row and line.
